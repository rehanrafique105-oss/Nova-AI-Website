Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Nova AI.

What is Nova AI?

Nova AI is a modern AI assistant designed to help with learning, productivity, writing, coding and digital tasks.

Can I use Nova AI for learning?

Yes. Nova AI can help you understand topics, organize study plans and practice new skills.

Can Nova AI help with coding?

Yes. You can use the AI chat interface to learn programming concepts and get coding guidance.

Is Nova AI available on mobile?

The website is designed to work on both desktop and mobile screens.
